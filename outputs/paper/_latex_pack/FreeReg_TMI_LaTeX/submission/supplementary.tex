\textbf{VLM negative result.} Substituting a Qwen2-VL-2B vision tower (with
VLoRA) for the lightweight ViT gives no accuracy gain at equal budget (ADD-S
0.200 at 4 epochs versus the ViT's convergence to 0.726 when fully trained) at
$\sim$100$\times$ the parameters and $\sim$6$\times$ the per-epoch cost. A
general VLM's language-aligned features do not specifically benefit geometric
pose regression, and the larger model is harder to train on limited data.

\section{Observability analysis}
The single-view depth-residual Jacobian $\mathbf{J}=-\mathbf{W}\otimes\mathbf{R}\mathbf{z}$
is severely rank-deficient: only 151 of 648 singular values exceed
$\sigma_{\max}\cdot10^{-3}$ (23\%). The unobservable null space includes tubular
roll, tangential surface flow, and smooth low-frequency warps that preserve the
sampled depth profile. The spectral projection $\mathbf{V}_{r}\mathbf{V}_{r}^{\top}$
in the RTW objective restricts deformation to the observable subspace, both
stabilizing optimization and preventing hallucinated structure.

\textbf{Ray-sliding counterexample.} A determinant-corrupting rotation
parameterization bug ($\mathbf{K}=\mathbf{K}-\mathbf{K}^{\top}$ doubling an
antisymmetric skew matrix, $\det\mathbf{R}\approx49$) caused the depth residual
to converge to 0.002 while shape error reached 268\%: motion in the
unobservable subspace is invisible to the residual, so the optimizer corrupts
geometry while reporting success. We recommend determinant/orthonormality
assertions as first-class invariants in differentiable SE(3)/SO(3) code.

\textbf{Silhouette-IoU sensitivity to registration error} (AR fusion fidelity).
On the airway model, silhouette IoU between the registered and ground-truth
render falls from 1.00 (no error) to 0.77 (0.5\,mm/0.3$\degree$), 0.62
(1\,mm/0.6$\degree$), 0.44 (2\,mm/1.2$\degree$), and 0.32 (4\,mm/2.3$\degree$);
refinement halves the photometric residual from 0.325 to 0.163. This couples
render fidelity directly to registration accuracy, supporting the residual as
an online validation signal (Fig.~5).

\section{Real-video ground-truth annotation protocol}
\label{sec:sup_gt}
The main text evaluates quantitative clinical TRE on real-CT synthetic
fly-throughs with ground-truth poses; real intra-operative video is used for
qualitative AR demonstration. To prepare real-video quantitative validation, we
built a dual-reader annotation protocol: FreeReg proposes centerline-projected
junctions on each frame, two readers independently click the corresponding
anatomical landmark (carina, main/lobar bronchi), and a consensus step accepts
pairs agreeing within 15\,px (taking the mean) and flags the rest for
adjudication. On the pilot annotation batch, both readers produced 30
landmarks across 5 airway labels; 100\% of pairs agreed within the 15\,px
tolerance, with a mean inter-reader discrepancy of 6.6\,px (median 6.0\,px).
This establishes that the annotation workflow is consistent enough to support a
future dual-reader ground-truth study; we report it here as protocol readiness
rather than as a validated clinical accuracy claim.
