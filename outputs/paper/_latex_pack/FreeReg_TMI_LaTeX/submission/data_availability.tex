\begin{center}\textbf{Data Availability Statement}\end{center}

The public benchmarks used in this study are available from their original
providers: BOP Linemod and T-LESS (BOP benchmark), SCARED (endoscopic stereo),
real-colon FEM, EndoNeRF, and BlendedMVS. The FoundationPose and Endo3R
implementations compared against are publicly released by their respective
teams; we use our Windows port of FoundationPose for the full-benchmark
comparison.

The clinical CT-to-bronchoscopy data are de-identified patient records provided
by Henan Provincial People's Hospital and are not publicly available owing to
institutional data-governance restrictions; access may be requested from the
hospital subject to its data-governance process.

All evaluation scripts, the Windows build patches for FoundationPose/Endo3R,
and the result figures across every dataset are released by the authors to
support reproducibility.
