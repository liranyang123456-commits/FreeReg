Cover Letter — IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Editorial Board,

We submit our manuscript entitled "FreeReg: Free-Coordinate Registration of
Arbitrary 3D Models to Arbitrary Image Streams for Augmented and Mixed Reality"
for consideration in IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging.

PROBLEM AND MOTIVATION
Augmented and mixed reality require registering an arbitrary 3D model, given in
its own coordinates, onto an arbitrary image or video stream. The classical
calibrate-then-align pipeline assumes a calibrated camera, a rigid target, and
independent per-frame poses — assumptions that fail under uncalibrated optics,
tissue deformation, and temporal jitter, all of which are the norm in
image-guided intervention.

CONTRIBUTIONS
We formulate registration as free-coordinate estimation of the projection
mapping among model, camera, and pixel frames, with intrinsics, depth, rigid
pose, and deformation each optional and degradable. FreeReg combines:
  (i) a multi-source correspondence engine with contour-gated arbitration;
 (ii) an observability-guided rigid-plus-warp estimator that deforms only
      within the spectrally observable subspace of the single-view
      depth-residual operator (rank-deficient to 23%);
(iii) a manifold SE(3) Kalman filter with innovation gating; and
 (iv) a co-anchored registration-rendering field whose photometric residual
      provides online, label-free validation.

We validate one and the same engine across five domains — rigid CAD (BOP
Linemod/T-LESS), deformable tissue (real-colon FEM), and clinical
CT-to-bronchoscopy and SCARED endoscopy — matching or exceeding SOTA where they
apply (BOP AR 0.848 vs FoundationPose 0.847; T-LESS ADD-S 0.921) and remaining
correct where general-purpose methods collapse out-of-distribution (on clinical
endoscopy FoundationPose reaches 104 mm and Endo3R 34 mm, while FreeReg reaches
4.23 mm mean TRE at 30-80 FPS, within the accuracy range of electromagnetic
navigation without external tracking hardware). The observability analysis gives
explicit reliability limits and shows a second view nearly doubles the
recoverable subspace (23% to 41%).

RELEVANCE TO IEEE TMI
Image-guided intervention is a central TMI topic, and registration is its
recognized bottleneck. FreeReg targets camera-only, calibration-free
registration for bronchoscopy and endoscopy — directly addressing the accuracy
and robustness limits that prior reviews identify as blocking clinical adoption
of surgical AR — while its free-coordinate formulation and observability
analysis are of broad methodological interest to the medical-imaging community.
