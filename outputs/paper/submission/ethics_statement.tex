\begin{center}\textbf{Ethics Statement}\end{center}

This study is a retrospective secondary analysis of de-identified clinical
imaging (CT volumes and intra-operative bronchoscopy videos). The clinical data
were de-identified by the responsible hospital department through its strict
institutional review process and were provided by Dr. Nan Wei. Data access and
use were reviewed and authorised through the institutional data-governance
process of Henan Provincial People's Hospital. No animal experiments,
prospective enrolment, research-directed intervention, or identifiable
participant information were involved, and the study complies with the
Declaration of Helsinki.

Public benchmarks (BOP Linemod/T-LESS, SCARED, real-colon FEM, EndoNeRF,
BlendedMVS) are used under their original providers' access terms; these
contain no new human or animal material acquired for this study. The clinical
CT-to-bronchoscopy evaluation uses de-identified patient CT and intra-operative
video provided by Henan Provincial People's Hospital under the authorisation
described above.
