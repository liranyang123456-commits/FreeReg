\section*{1. Study type}
This is a retrospective secondary analysis of existing, de-identified clinical
imaging data (preoperative CT volumes and intra-operative bronchoscopy video
frames). The study involves no prospective enrolment of participants, no
research-directed intervention, no animal experiments, and no collection of
identifiable private information.

\section*{2. Source and custodianship of the clinical data}
The clinical CT-to-bronchoscopy data were acquired at Henan Provincial
People's Hospital (Zhengzhou, China) in the course of routine clinical care,
and were de-identified by the responsible hospital department through its
standard institutional review procedure prior to any research access. The data
were made available to the research team by Dr.~Nan Wei (co-author, Henan
Provincial People's Hospital).

\section*{3. Ethics approval / oversight}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Institutional review.} Data access and use for this retrospective
  analysis were reviewed and authorised through the institutional
  data-governance and ethics-review process of Henan Provincial People's
  Hospital.
  \item \textbf{Approval / waiver reference:} Not applicable --- retrospective
  analysis of de-identified data conducted under the institutional
  data-governance authorisation of Henan Provincial People's Hospital.
\item \textbf{Informed consent.} Requirement for individual informed consent was
  not required, as the study is a retrospective analysis of de-identified data
  with no participant contact or intervention.
\item \textbf{Declaration of Helsinki.} The study was conducted in accordance
  with the ethical principles of the Declaration of Helsinki (2013 revision).
\end{itemize}

\section*{4. Public benchmark data}
Publicly released benchmarks used for validation (BOP Linemod/T-LESS, SCARED,
the real-colon FEM benchmark, EndoNeRF, BlendedMVS) were used under their
original providers' access and licence terms. These resources contain no new
human or animal material acquired for the present study.

\section*{5. Data availability}
The de-identified clinical imaging data are subject to the data-governance
policy of Henan Provincial People's Hospital and are not publicly
redistributable. Derived evaluation scripts and the software pipeline are
released by the authors for reproducibility, as described in the manuscript.
